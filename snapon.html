What I built at Snap-on

Two captive businesses inside a company on the S&P 500. A global finance arm, and two insurance companies, both serving the same franchise network.

The finance captive

A lending business across four countries: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia and New Zealand. It financed franchisees carrying their vans and stock, and the technicians buying from them. Snap-on reports it as a separate segment, so every figure below is public.

Financial services segment, 2022
Share of group revenue~6%
Share of group operating earnings~22%
Segment operating earnings$266m
Gross finance portfolio$2,278m
Delinquency, 60 days and over, US1.3%

What that means

A fifth of group operating profit on six per cent of revenue. The share of earnings is calculated from the reported segment figures rather than taken from a line in the accounts, and the arithmetic is in the note alongside.

The portfolio grew from $733m in 2010 to $2,278m in 2022. Delinquency held flat while it grew. Growth without deterioration is the harder half.

The insurance captives

Two insurance companies covering franchisees across auto, inventory and product liability. This sat inside the Tools segment, so there are no separate public figures, and I do not publish any.

What I can tell you is how members behaved. Participation was voluntary throughout.

Franchise insurance program, my own figures
Franchisee participation85%
Retention~100%
People kept choosing it, year after year. That is the only real evidence a network program serves its members rather than earns from them.

Captives for franchise networks

Where to checkSnap-on Incorporated fourth quarter and full year 2022 results, and the Form 10-K. Segment figures appear in both.
The arithmeticOperating earnings before financial services ran at 20.9% of $4,492.8m in net sales, about $939m. Financial services added $266m. That puts the segment at roughly 22% of the total.
RolesHired in 2010 as Vice President, Financial Services, responsible for the global captive finance business and Treasury. In 2014 the remit expanded to all insurance, including the two captive insurance companies, and the title President of Snap-on SecureCorp came with it.
Why only one set of figuresThe finance captive is a reported segment. The insurance captives were not separately reported.
